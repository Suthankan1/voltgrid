# Participant Instructions

Thank you for helping evaluate the **VoltGrid Operator Console**.

## What we are testing
We are testing **the interface**, not you. If something is unclear, hard to find, or confusingly worded, that is valuable feedback for us to fix.

## How the session works
1. We will ask you to complete **9 short operational tasks** plus a few brief understanding questions.
2. **Think aloud**: While you work, please say what you are looking at, what you expect to happen, and anything that surprises or confuses you.
3. Each task starts on a specific screen. Please wait for the moderator to say **"Start"** before clicking, and let the moderator know as soon as you believe you have found the answer or finished the step.
4. During timed tasks, the moderator will remain quiet and will not give hints unless you are stuck and request assistance (assisted completions are recorded separately).
5. You may pause or stop at any time. **No personal, customer, or live production data** is used—every station and session shown is simulated local test data.

## Consent Check
Before starting:
- Confirm you consent to anonymized notes, task timings, and usability survey scores being recorded for VoltGrid quality review.
- Confirm you are comfortable using the keyboard/mouse/touchscreen setup in front of you.
